Adversarial: 99.3% rescored across 10 conversations. Adversarial questions swap speakers to induce hallucination. Calvin & Dave hits 100% rescored adversarial (46/46) — seventh conversation to do so. Open-domain: 88.5% rescored. The retrieval prompt explicitly instructs "Answer only from what you find in the graph. Never use your own knowledge to fill gaps." Open-domain accuracy under that constraint excludes 20 questions where the expected answer requires latent LLM knowledge — geographic bridges (Tampa→Florida, Smoky Mts→NC/TN, Stamford→Connecticut, Phuket-as-dive-spot, Voyageurs→Minnesota, Lake Tahoe→California), cultural/catalog bridges, domain expertise, or clinical/demographic inference. Calvin & Dave hits 100% rescored open-domain (7/7) — third conversation to do so after Tim & John and Audrey & Andrew.

Single-hop: 81.6% rescored (weakest). Calvin & Dave's only single-hop miss (Q60) is itself a list-completeness case (necklace identified, custom guitar gift missed). The over-counting and strict-inference patterns from earlier conversations also persist. See Error Analysis below.

Architecture​

Morpheus's three-branch memory graph is motivated by two foundational theories from cognitive science:

  • Tulving's memory classification (1972) distinguishes episodic memory (personal events situated in time and place) from semantic memory (general knowledge detached from specific episodes). Morpheus adds a third branch — procedural — following the broader taxonomy that includes "knowing how" alongside "knowing what" and "remembering when."
  • Complementary Learning Systems theory (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly, 1995) argues that effective memory requires two complementary systems: a fast-learning system that captures specific episodes, and a slow-learning system that gradually extracts structure. In Morpheus, the episodic branch captures events as they occur (one fact per node, organized by date), while the semantic branch consolidates facts and relationships across conversations over time. Cross-branch LINK edges (involves, instance_of, learned_from) serve as the binding mechanism between the two systems.
Root
├── Semantic — facts, entities, relationships (by topic)
├── Episodic — events and activities (by date)
└── Procedural — workflows and procedures (by domain)

Ingestion: Opus 4.7 (max effort), one session at a time, one fact per leaf. Episodic events as flat siblings under dates, chained with continues links. Procedural concepts auto-spawn Pitfalls children.

Retrieval (Fly Swatter Search): 5 FTS5 + 5 vector results, plus top 20 neighborhood nodes ranked by embedding similarity. get-node provides query-ranked neighbors and ±14-day episodic windows.

Limitations​

  • Full LOCOMO suite (10 of 10 conversations, 1977 questions).
  • Different judge model — Claude Opus 4.7 vs gpt-4.1-mini for other systems.
  • One-pass ingestion — multi-pass reinforcement would improve cross-concept linking and single-hop completeness.
  • Image-dependent questions — some details require visual recognition.

Note on One-Pass Graph Generation​

These results are from a single ingestion pass — each session processed once, graph never revisited. In practice, important concepts are reinforced across recurring conversations. We expect single-hop to improve as cross-concept links are established through topic recurrence.
